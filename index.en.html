Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes the policies that Purpleye follows when handling personal or equivalent information of users (hereinafter referred to as "Users") of the app "Chill Fire" (hereinafter referred to as "the App").

■Scope of Application

This privacy policy applies only within the App.

■Collection of Personal Information

The App respects the privacy of its Users and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or location data. There is no account registration.

Settings such as volume, campfire intensity, and game progress are stored only on the device and are not sent elsewhere.

The App does not provide any such information to third parties.

■Advertisements and Analytics

No advertisements are displayed in the App. The App does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

■Changes to Privacy Policy

The App complies with applicable Japanese laws regarding personal information and will continuously review and improve this privacy policy. The revised policy will always be disclosed on this page.
